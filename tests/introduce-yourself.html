🗨️ Grammar

Introduce Yourself

Can-do goal: Introduce yourself and ask basic questions using am, is, and are.
Warm-up
Before we start...You meet a new classmate. Say three facts about yourself: your name, where you are from, and how you feel today. Which form of to be do you need?
Useful introduction phrases
PhraseMeaningExample
I'm ...my name / identity"I'm Danylo."
I'm from ...place of origin"I'm from Ukraine."
This is ...introducing another person"This is my friend, Olha."
Are you ...?asking a yes/no question"Are you new here?"
Nice to meet you.first meeting"Nice to meet you, too!"
Remember: I'm is the short form of I am. Use it naturally when you speak: "I'm Maria. I'm 12."
Grammar: the verb to be
SubjectForm of to beExample
Iam / 'mI am Anton. / I'm Anton.
he / she / itis / 'sShe is my classmate. / She's my classmate.
you / we / theyare / 'reWe are in class 6A. / We're in class 6A.
Question word order: put am/is/are before the subject: "Are you new?" "Is she your friend?"
Mini-dialogue

Oleh: Hi! I'm Oleh. What's your name?

Sofia: Hello, I'm Sofia. Nice to meet you.

Oleh: Nice to meet you, too. Are you new here?

Sofia: Yes, I am. I'm from Lviv. Are you from Kyiv?

Oleh: Yes, I am. We are in the same class!

Practice

✎ Homework

  1. Write a 6-line dialogue where two new classmates introduce themselves. Use am, is, and are.
  2. Write five true sentences about yourself using I'm.
  3. Practise the dialogue aloud twice, then ask a partner: "Are you new here?"
